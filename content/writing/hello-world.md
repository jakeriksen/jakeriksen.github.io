Hello, my name is Aleksander Eriksen. I have been coding for about eight years
now (per 2026).

I have an MSc in mechanical engineering, and spent most of my thesis teaching a
computer to design water chambers. Those are the concrete boxes under the road
that nobody thinks about until something goes wrong, and I know more about them
than I ever planned to. It turned into a company, Cadea, backed with 1M NOK in
funding from Innovation Norway.

Two products later I am doing much the same thing, just with accounting instead
of concrete: find something tedious, and try to get a computer to do it
properly.

Away from the keyboard I cook, watch more films and series than I should, and
play guitar. I draw once in a while too. Looking at that list, I think what I
actually like is the creative part, which is probably why building products is
the piece of the work I enjoy most.
